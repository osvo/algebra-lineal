Six ways to see the invisible.

Start in the plane, shift your perspective, and finish by breaking linearity. Every module responds immediately to your input.

01 / FOUNDATION

2D transformations

Deform the plane with a 2 × 2 matrix and study its determinant, eigenvectors, image, and kernel.

→ 02 / COORDINATES

Change of basis

Describe the same vector in two coordinate systems and understand what remains invariant.

→ 03 / ORDER

Composition

Chain two transformations, reverse their order, and see why matrix multiplication does not commute.

→ 04 / SPACE

3D transformations

Rotate, project, and reflect space from different viewpoints with three-dimensional controls.

→ 05 / DIMENSION

Non-square matrices

Watch a transformation move between dimensions and see what rank and nullity reveal.

→ 06 / CONTRAST

Nonlinear transformations

Compare linear behavior with transformations that bend, ripple, or distort the plane.

→

Do not memorize first. Manipulate, observe, then name.

The laboratory is a space for exploration: every control changes a mathematical representation, and every visual turns the result into something you can inspect.
